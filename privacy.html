Privacy Policy

KnB stats is an internal tool operated by Kons na Bis business club to collect performance statistics (views, watch time, audience data) from YouTube channels that the club itself owns and manages.

We access this data via the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, using OAuth authorization granted directly by the channel owner. Data is used solely for internal reporting and is not shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.

Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.
